An admin list can be narrowed by date. Mark a `date` or `datetime` field `filterable()` and its toolbar offers a From and a To day. Days are whole, inclusive, and start at the reader's midnight. The range rides along on the pager, the sort headers, the filter links' counts, the export and a row's Back link. In the skeleton, ten lists use it: Activity, Audit, Mail, Failed jobs, Scheduled runs, Users, Sessions, Access tokens, Logs and Files. That is the question a running instance gets asked first: what happened yesterday?

It is 0.25.0 because the admin gains API: `DateRange`, `Criteria::$ranges`, `TableSource`'s `unixTime:` argument and `SourceContractTestCase::rangeValues()`. Nothing was taken away.

## Upgrading

- **The admin:** a source whose test extends `SourceContractTestCase` and that adds a timestamp to its `filterable` list must give it a range in `rangeValues()` (`['created_at' => $this->days('2026-10-02', '2026-10-03')]`), or the case reports the filter as unchecked. A source that writes its own SQL, or holds its rows in memory, reads `$criteria->ranges` itself: `condition($column)` (pass `'U'` for unix seconds) or `contains($instant)`. If you shadow `views/admin/partials/filters.php`, copy the new date-range block and the form's `change delay:250ms` trigger from the package's copy.
- **The skeleton:** to pick up the ten lists, copy the `->filterable()` on each module's timestamp field and the matching `filterable` (and, for INT columns, `unixTime`) entries on its source.

## The admin

- A list can be narrowed by date. `filterable()` on a `date` or `datetime` field now draws a From and a To day in the toolbar instead of an exact match, read as `{field}_from` / `{field}_to` in the URL. Both ends are whole days in the reader's zone, either can be left open, and the range carries into the pager, the export and the filter links' counts. `Hydra\Admin\DateRange` does the reading: `condition()` for a source that writes SQL, `contains()` for one that holds its rows in memory. A `TableSource` whose timestamp is an INT of unix seconds names it in the new `unixTime:` argument, so the range binds seconds. `SourceContractTestCase` gains `rangeValues()`, and holds a filterable timestamp to narrowing the way it holds any other filter.

## The skeleton

- Activity, Audit, Mail, Failed jobs, Scheduled runs, Users, Sessions, Access tokens, Logs and Files filter by day. Mail, Failed jobs and Scheduled runs name their INT timestamps in `unixTime:`. Sessions and Access tokens read the range in their own SQL, and Logs reads it through `contains()`. Rate limits, Jobs and Scheduler have none, because their rows only cover minutes, or are one per task.
